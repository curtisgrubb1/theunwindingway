3. FUNCTIONS AND TARGET AUDIENCE
The Way is a 365-day contemplative practice app. It presents one lesson per day from A Course in Miracles, in sequence, and provides an I Ching oracle to consult when a question is unclear. Readings are saved to a private journal stored on the device.
The audience is adults already engaged with contemplative practice. The problem it solves is continuity: these traditions ask for daily attention over a long period, and people lose their place. The app keeps the sequence, holds the record of what was consulted, and makes the day's material available without a book, a bookmark, or an internet connection.
The app makes no health, medical, therapeutic, or wellness claims, and provides no advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

4. SETUP AND ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS
No setup, login, credentials, or sample files are required. The app is fully functional on install.
- Daily lesson: shown on launch. "Continue" advances through it.
- Oracle: choose to consult from the main screen. Six lines form one at a time, then the resolved hexagram and its reading are displayed.
- Journal: readings save automatically and can be viewed or deleted. Private to the device, never transmitted.
- Daily reminder: under About. Off by default. Enabling it requests notification permission and schedules a local notification at a chosen time.
- Widget: long-press the home screen and search for "The Way."
To reach any lesson directly, tap the lesson number at the top of the lesson screen and enter a number from 1 to 365.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS, AND PLATFORMS
None. The app makes no network requests of any kind.
All content, fonts, and code are contained in the app bundle. There is no backend, API, data provider, authentication service, payment processor, analytics, advertising SDK, crash reporting, or AI service. No user data is transmitted anywhere, which is why the privacy label is Data Not Collected. The app is built with Capacitor, an open-source native runtime. All state is stored locally on the device.
