Where your practice is kept

On your device, and nowhere else. There is no account and no backup. Deleting the app deletes your progress, your journal, and every reading you have saved.

Moving to a new device

Your practice does not travel with you. Note the day you are on before you leave the old device, and set it again on the new one.

The daily reminder

It is off unless you turn it on, under About. If you have turned it on and nothing arrives, check that notifications are permitted for The Way in your device settings.

Working without a connection

The app is complete on your device. It needs no network at all — not to open a lesson, not to consult the oracle, not to write in the journal.
